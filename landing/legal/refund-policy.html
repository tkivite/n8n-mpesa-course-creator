We offer a 30-day refund window for first-time purchases of n8n M-Pesa Mastery. If the course is not a fit, you may request a refund within 30 calendar days of purchase, subject to the conditions below.

2. Eligible purchases

3. Refund conditions

  • The request is made within 30 days of purchase
  • The purchase can be verified using a receipt, order number, or payment email
