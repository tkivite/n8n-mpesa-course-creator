# Privacy Policy : n8n M-Pesa Mastery

> Last updated: 2026-10-03

This privacy policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected in connection with **n8n M-Pesa Mastery**, its sales pages, checkout flows, private repository access process, and support operations.

This course is offered by **iTik Solutions**.

> This is a practical course-business privacy policy template and should be reviewed for your exact legal and tax setup before public launch.

---

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to people who:

- visit the course landing page
- purchase the course
- request access to the private repository
- contact support
- join an associated community or mailing list

---

## 2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the course, we may collect:

- name
- email address
- GitHub username
- payment / order identifiers
- purchase tier
- support messages
- device or browser metadata from analytics or logs
- IP address or request metadata for access-control and abuse prevention

We do **not** need your M-Pesa PIN or your private Safaricom login details to deliver the course.

---

## 3. How information is collected

Information may be collected through:

- checkout platforms such as Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or similar providers
- forms used to claim private repo access
- support email conversations
- community signup forms
- basic server logs for security and access-control purposes

---

## 4. Why information is used

Information is used to:

- process and verify purchases
- deliver the correct course tier
- grant or revoke private repo access
- respond to support requests
- prevent fraud, chargeback abuse, and unauthorized access
- improve course delivery and documentation
- comply with legal, accounting, and tax obligations

---

## 5. Private repo access data

If your purchase includes private repository access, your GitHub username may be stored together with your purchase record to:

- invite you to the repo
- verify seat usage
- revoke access after a refund or policy violation
- audit suspicious access attempts

---

## 6. Payment processing

Payments are normally processed by third-party providers such as Gumroad or similar checkout services.

We do not store your full card details directly unless a separate payment processor explicitly shows otherwise.

Please also review the privacy policy of the checkout provider you use.

---

## 7. Data sharing

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

- payment processors
- email providers
- GitHub, for private repo invitation workflows
- support tools or community platforms you choose to join
- legal, accounting, or regulatory authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 8. Data retention

We may retain relevant purchase and support records for legitimate business, tax, security, and compliance reasons.

Typical retention may include:

- purchase records
- access logs
- support conversations
- repo-access audit logs

Retention periods should be reviewed against your local legal and tax obligations.

---

## 9. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect course-related data, including:

- limiting private repo access to verified buyers
- revoking access after refunds where applicable
- protecting admin credentials and tokens
- using HTTPS where available
- restricting access to operational systems where practical

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

---

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request:

- access to the personal data held about you
- correction of inaccurate data
- deletion of data where legally allowed
- clarification on how your data is used

Some requests may be limited where data must be kept for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or contractual reasons.

## 11. Minors

This course is intended for adults or learners old enough to purchase and use technical training products in their jurisdiction.

If you believe personal data was submitted by someone who should not have done so, contact support.

## 12. Policy updates

This privacy policy may be updated over time as the course business, tooling, or legal requirements change.

The latest version should always be the one published on the current landing page or distributed documentation.
