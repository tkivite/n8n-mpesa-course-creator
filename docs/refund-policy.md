> Last updated: 2026-10-03

This course is offered by **iTik Solutions**.

---

## 1. Summary

We offer a **30-day refund window** for first-time purchases of the course.

If the course is not a fit, you may request a refund within 30 calendar days of purchase, subject to the conditions below.

---

## 2. Eligible purchases

The refund policy applies to:

- Starter tier
- Pro tier
- Agency tier
- direct checkout purchases made through the approved sales page or checkout link

---

## 3. Conditions for a refund

A refund request is normally approved if all of the following are true:

- the request is made within 30 days of purchase
- the purchase can be verified using the checkout receipt, order number, or payment email
- the request comes from the original buyer
- the buyer has not violated the course access or acceptable-use terms

---

## 4. Non-refundable situations

A refund may be refused in the following situations:

- the request is made more than 30 days after purchase
- the purchase cannot be verified
- the buyer has shared private repo access, download links, or course files with others
- the buyer initiated a chargeback instead of contacting support first
- the buyer is requesting a second refund after a prior refund for the same product line
- the purchase was part of a custom consulting, implementation, or done-for-you service agreement unless separately stated in writing

---

## 5. What happens after a refund

If a refund is approved:

- access to the private consumer repository may be revoked
- any private community or premium access granted as part of the purchase may be revoked
- future product updates tied to that purchase may no longer be available

If you downloaded files before the refund, you must stop using them after the refund is completed.
