> **Lesson 10.1 : Packaging & pricing**

### [VO]
> Welcome to module ten. The agency tier module. If you plan to sell M-Pesa integration services to Kenyan businesses, this module shows you how to package, price, and deliver repeatably. No consulting fluff — numbers and templates.

### [SCREEN] 0:15–1:30
Three-tier package structure.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 0:15–1:30
> **Three tiers:**
> Starter · 25,000 KES
> Standard · 75,000 KES
> Premium · 200,000 KES

### [VO]
> Three tiers. Starter, Standard, Premium. Prices for Kenyan businesses. Twenty-five thousand, seventy-five thousand, two hundred thousand shillings respectively.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Three tiers because of the "goldilocks" effect. Most buyers pick middle. If you offer only one price, people negotiate. If you offer three, most say "I'll take the standard".

### [SCREEN] 1:30–3:00
Starter tier deliverables.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 1:30–3:00
> **Starter: 25,000 KES**
> ✓ n8n installed on client's VPS
> ✓ 4 core workflows deployed
> ✓ Sandbox + production credentials wired
> ✓ Basic documentation handed over
> ✓ 7-day defect warranty

### [VO]
> Starter: twenty-five thousand. Covers everything you learned in modules one through five. Install n8n on their VPS. Deploy the four workflows. Wire production credentials. Hand over a one-page runbook. Seven-day warranty.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Target customer: a founder with a technical team who can take it from here. You're basically selling them the fast start.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Your effort: six to eight hours. Hourly rate implied: three thousand plus.

## Lesson 10.2 : Client onboarding checklist *(7 min)*

### [SCREEN] 0:00–0:15
Title card.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 0:00–0:10
> **Lesson 10.2 : Repeatable onboarding**

### [VO]
> Delivering twenty M-Pesa integrations profitably means doing them the same way every time. Here's the checklist I use. Ten items. From signed proposal to go-live in five business days.

### [SCREEN] 0:15–1:30
Day 1: Discovery call.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 0:15–1:30
> **Day 1: 45-min discovery call**
> • Business model
> • Volume (TX/day projected)
> • Current M-Pesa setup (manual? other?)
> • Integration targets (CRM, inventory, email)
> • Timeline, decision-maker, budget

### [VO]
> Day one. Forty-five-minute call. Ask about business model, projected volume, current M-Pesa handling if any, integration targets, timeline, budget.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> One slide at the end of the call: here's what I recommend, here's the tier, here's the price. If they say yes verbally, send the proposal within two hours. Momentum.

### [SCREEN] 1:30–3:00
Day 2: Credentials and access handover.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 1:30–3:00
> **Day 2: Secure handover**
> • NDA (if sensitive)
> • Daraja production credentials
> • Domain & DNS access OR
> &nbsp;&nbsp;provision VPS yourself
> • Access to target systems (Sheets, CRM, etc.)

### [VO]
> Day two. NDA first if they haven't shared production credentials before. Then Daraja production Consumer Key, Secret, Passkey, Shortcode. Domain and DNS access, or you provision the VPS yourself and charge for it.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Use a secure channel for secrets. 1Password shared vault, Bitwarden Send, or at minimum encrypted email. Never Slack DMs or regular email.

### [SCREEN] 3:00–4:30
Day 3-4: Build and deploy.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 3:00–4:30
> **Days 3–4: Build**
> • Provision VPS, run `vps-setup.sh`
> • Import workflows from the course repo
> • Customize per their use case
> • Internal testing with sandbox
> • One real production test (1 KES)

### [VO]
> Days three and four. VPS provisioning. Workflow import. Customization. Internal testing. One real production test with one shilling.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Keep a per-client git repo private fork of the course repo. Any customization goes there. Commit, push, tag. If the client's workflow ever breaks, you can diff against the baseline and know what changed.

### [SCREEN] 4:30–5:30
Day 5: Training and handover.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 4:30–5:30
> **Day 5: Handover**
> • 60-min developer walkthrough
> • Runbook delivery
> • Monitoring dashboard tour
> • Warranty + support terms
> • Payment collected

### [VO]
> Day five. Sixty-minute walkthrough with their lead developer. Walk them through each workflow. Walk them through the monitoring dashboard. Hand over the runbook. Confirm warranty and support terms. Collect payment.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Important: collect payment on day five, not later. Deliver value. Ask for payment. Most clients pay same-day if the delivery is clean.

### [SCREEN] 5:30–6:30
Common pitfalls.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 5:30–6:30
> **Scope creep traps:**
> • "Can you also add..." → Change order, new price
> • "Can we test for one more week" → Clock starts regardless
> • "Oh we need SMS too" → New workflow = +25,000

### [VO]
> Scope creep traps to avoid. "Can you add just one more thing" — treat as a change order with a new price. "Can we test for another week" — the warranty clock starts regardless. "We also need SMS" — great, that's a new workflow at twenty-five thousand.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Be friendly but firm. Clients respect clear boundaries. The ones who don't aren't clients you want.

### [SCREEN] 6:30–7:00
End card.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 6:30–7:00
> **Up next:** White-labeling your delivery

### [VO]
> Onboarding done. Final lesson: how to white-label and resell. See you there.

## Lesson 10.3 : White-labeling and scaling *(6 min)*

### [SCREEN] 0:00–0:15
Title card.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 0:00–0:10
> **Lesson 10.3 : White-label & scale**

### [VO]
> Final lesson. Three strategies to scale beyond "one developer selling time". Each is a different business model.

### [SCREEN] 0:15–1:30
Strategy 1: productize per vertical.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 0:15–1:30
> **Strategy 1: Vertical products**
> • "M-Pesa for Schools" package: 150k KES
> • "M-Pesa for SACCOs" package: 300k KES
> • "M-Pesa for E-commerce": 100k KES

### [VO]
> Strategy one: productize per vertical. Instead of custom integrations, build three fixed packages. School. SACCO. E-commerce. Each one bundles the specific workflows for that vertical plus their quirks — fees receipts for schools, loan repayment matching for SACCOs, Woo integration for e-commerce.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Higher margin because repeatable. Lower risk because the scope is known. You can finish a school integration in two days if you've done ten before.

### [SCREEN] 1:30–3:00
Strategy 2: resell to other agencies.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 1:30–3:00
> **Strategy 2: White-label**
> License your workflows + playbook to:
> • WordPress / dev agencies
> • Hosting companies
> • Software consultancies

### [VO]
> Strategy two: white-label. Many Kenyan dev agencies serve clients but don't have M-Pesa expertise. License your workflows and playbook to them.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Pricing: five hundred thousand upfront plus five percent of their deliverables. They sell to their clients at full price, you don't touch client-facing work.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Three to five white-label partners and you have a seven-figure business without ever doing another client call yourself.

### [SCREEN] 3:00–4:30
Strategy 3: SaaS product.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 3:00–4:30
> **Strategy 3: Turn it into SaaS**
> Multi-tenant n8n instance you run.
> Clients sign up, enter their Daraja creds,
> use pre-built workflows via dashboard.
> Charge monthly: 5-15k KES/client

### [VO]
> Strategy three: turn it into a SaaS. Multi-tenant n8n instance you host. Clients sign up, enter their Daraja credentials, pick workflows from a catalog, pay monthly.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Harder to build. Much higher ceiling. If you can get a hundred SMBs paying ten thousand a month each, that's a million shillings monthly recurring. That's a real company.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> Technical hurdle: multi-tenant isolation in n8n. There's a paid n8n license for embedding. Alternatively, run one n8n container per tenant on a shared VPS — cheap but you have to manage upgrades carefully.

### [SCREEN] 4:30–5:30
Final sales advice.

### [ON-SCREEN TEXT] 4:30–5:30
> **Sales channels (Kenya):**
> ✅ LinkedIn (highest conversion for B2B)
> ✅ WhatsApp Business groups
> ✅ Guest posts on Medium / Dev.to
> ✅ YouTube build-in-public videos

### [VO]
> Where to find clients. In Kenya, LinkedIn is the single highest-converting channel for B2B services. Post weekly about M-Pesa integrations you've shipped. Tag clients when they allow.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> WhatsApp Business groups where founders hang out. Guest posts on Medium or Dev.to for inbound SEO. YouTube videos showing you building — build-in-public style, three minutes each, consistent posting.
>
> [PAUSE 1s]
>
> One post a week consistently is better than ten posts in a burst. Momentum matters.
