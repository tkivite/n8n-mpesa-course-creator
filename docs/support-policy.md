# Support Policy : n8n M-Pesa Mastery

> Last updated: 2026-10-03

This support policy explains what buyers of **n8n M-Pesa Mastery** can expect when asking for help.

---

## 1. Supported platforms

This course is officially supported on:

- **macOS**
- **Windows**
- **Linux**

The course is built around Git, Docker, a browser, Postman, and local configuration using `.env` files.

---

## 3. What support covers

Support normally includes help with:

- accessing the course after purchase
- private repo invitation or access issues
- finding the correct setup guide for macOS, Windows, or Linux
- obvious broken links or missing files in the course materials
- local Docker startup problems related to the documented setup
- importing the provided n8n workflows
- importing the provided Postman collection
- understanding which course file to use at each stage

---

## 4. What support does not include

Support does **not** normally include:

- custom consulting for your business or client project
- debugging your unrelated production environment
- modifying the workflows for your unique business rules for free
- installing tools on locked-down corporate devices you do not control
- troubleshooting unsupported third-party infrastructure not covered in the course
- full one-on-one implementation help unless your tier or separate agreement includes it

---

## 5. Response targets

Target support response times:

- **Business days:** within 1–3 business days
- **Weekends / holidays:** next business day or later depending on workload

These are response targets, not guaranteed SLAs, unless separately agreed in writing.

---

## 6. Best way to ask for help

To get help faster, include:

- your operating system (`macOS`, `Windows`, or `Linux`)
- the exact step you are on
- the exact error message
- whether this is a setup, workflow, callback, Postman, or access issue
- screenshots if helpful

For technical issues, include the smallest reproducible detail possible.

---

## 7. Scope by tier

### Starter

Support is limited to:

- access issues
- broken file / broken link issues
- general setup guidance based on the published docs

### Pro

Support includes Starter support plus:

- consumer repo access issues
- clarification on included workflows and student assets

### Agency

Support may include additional help if explicitly promised in the checkout offer, such as:

- private consult sessions
- implementation guidance
- commercial packaging clarification

If Agency support is offered, define it clearly on the checkout page.
